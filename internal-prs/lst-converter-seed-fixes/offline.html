What changes in the converter output offline (event level, workflow 34634.711, 100 ttbar + 100 QCD events)

  • pT3/pT5 seeds: identical (offline pixel seeds carry no OT hits, so the hit-order fix has nothing to reorder); the few pT3/pT5 seeds that fall back to the pixel seed (7 per 100 ttbar events, 6 per 100 QCD events, out of about 160k) do so in both arms.
  • T5 candidates: identical in number.
  • T4: 133 (ttbar) / 113 (QCD) more T4 candidates per 100 events, +2.8% / +2.1% of all T4 candidates: these failed makeSeed in master (helix start on two hits of one module). The T4 seeds no longer carry the first-module strip hit.
  • Tracks: run-to-run, the reference reproduces itself exactly (100/100 ttbar events with identical track lists). With the fixes, about 5% of highPtTripletStep tracks seeded by unchanged pLS/pT3/pT5 seeds come out with sub-ppm parameter changes (same hits in 99.9% of them, median pT change 0, 90% below 2e-6): mkFit's result depends on the seed set it is given, and the T4 seeds changed.
